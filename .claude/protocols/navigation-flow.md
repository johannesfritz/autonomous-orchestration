# Navigation and Flow

**Purpose:** Provide guideposts so readers never guess where they are in the argument.
**Phase:** DRAFTING (while writing)
**Source:** World Bank Report Writing

---

## Why Navigation Matters

Analytical writing is complex. Without explicit guidance, readers:
- Lose track of the argument
- Miss connections between points
- Give up when structure isn't clear

**Your job:** Make the structure visible at every level.

---

## The Road Map

For pieces >1,500 words, the introduction must end with a **road map**.

### What It Is

A brief section describing how the document is organized—not just a list, but the **rationale** for the structure.

### Example

> This analysis proceeds in four parts. Section 1 establishes the baseline: where inflation stands today and why it matters. Section 2 examines the three drivers keeping it elevated—supply disruptions, central bank inaction, and wage pressures. Section 3 evaluates what current policy is doing (and what it's missing). Section 4 proposes a path forward, with specific recommendations for the Fed.

### Placement

End of introduction, before first body section.

### Checklist

- [ ] Road map present for posts >1,500 words
- [ ] States what each section covers
- [ ] Explains why this structure (not just lists topics)

## Explicit Transitions

Do not rely on readers to intuit connections. **Create the link explicitly.**

### Technique 1: The Hook

Begin a paragraph with a subordinate clause summarizing the previous paragraph, then introduce the new point in the main clause.

> "While supply chains explain the initial shock [summary of ¶ above], central bank inaction explains why it persists [new point]."

> "Although costs are a concern [previous point], the greater risk is reputational damage [new point]."

### Technique 2: The Setup

End a paragraph with a question that the next paragraph answers.

> "If supply chains are normalizing, why hasn't inflation fallen?"
>
> [Next paragraph begins:] "The answer lies in expectations..."

### Technique 3: Signpost Words

Use explicit connectors:

| Relationship | Words |
|--------------|-------|
| **Addition** | Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally |
| **Contrast** | However, Nevertheless, In contrast |
| **Cause** | Therefore, Consequently, As a result |
| **Example** | For instance, Specifically, Consider |
| **Sequence** | First, Second, Finally |

### Anti-Pattern

❌ **No transition:**
> Supply chains remain disrupted.
>
> Central banks have been slow to act.

✅ **Explicit transition:**
> Supply chains remain disrupted. **However, supply disruptions alone don't explain persistence.** Central bank inaction has allowed the shock to become entrenched.

## Forecasting Lists

When introducing a series of points, enumerate them **in advance**.

### Why

Readers need to know:
- How many items to expect
- What categories they fall into
- When the list is complete

### Examples

❌ **No forecast:**
> Several factors drive inflation...
> [Reader has no idea how many or when list ends]

✅ **Forecasted:**
> **Three factors** drive persistent inflation: supply disruptions, central bank inaction, and wage-price spirals.

❌ **Vague:**
> There are considerations on both sides.

✅ **Forecasted:**
> **Two arguments favor delay; three favor acting now.** The arguments for delay are...

### Rule

If you have 3+ points, enumerate upfront with a number.

## Section Headings as Signposts

Headings should be **informative**, not generic.

| Generic | Informative |
|---------|-------------|
| "Background" | "Where Inflation Stands Today" |
| "Analysis" | "Three Drivers of Persistent Inflation" |
| "Discussion" | "Why Current Policy Falls Short" |
| "Conclusion" | "What the Fed Should Do Next" |

**Rule:** A reader skimming headings should understand the argument's arc.

## Verification

After drafting, confirm:

- [ ] Road map present (if >1,500 words)
- [ ] Every section transition is explicit (hook, setup, or signpost)
- [ ] Lists are forecasted with numbers
- [ ] Headings are informative (not generic)

**Navigation failures are structural failures. Fix before proceeding to clarity revision.**
